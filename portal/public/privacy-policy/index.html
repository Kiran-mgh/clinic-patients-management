Privacy Policy

Amar Ayurveda (A Unit of Amar Hospital)

Last updated: August 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Amar Ayurveda (A Unit of Amar Hospital). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile application and web portal.

2. Information We Collect

We collect essential information required to verify patient identity, manage daily consultation tokens, and send queue status alerts:

  • Personal Identity Data: Full Name, Date of Birth, Gender, Town/Residence Area, Profession, and Blood Group.
  • Contact Information: Mobile Phone Number and Email Address.
  • Device & Notification Data: Device Push Tokens for delivering real-time queue position notifications (sound, vibration, and banner alerts).
  • Authentication Data: Encrypted password credentials and OTP verification sessions.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is strictly used for clinic operational and patient care purposes:

  • Authenticating patient registration and account logins via SMS / Email OTP.
  • Generating daily medicine consultation and treatment tokens.
  • Broadcasting real-time queue turn alerts ("It's Your Turn!", "1 Patient Ahead").
  • Linking mobile registrations with clinic medical records.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We implement strict security standards including SSL/TLS encryption. We DO NOT sell, trade, rent, or share your personal health data or contact information with third-party advertisers or external marketers.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active. Patients have the right to request deletion of their account and associated profile data at any time by contacting clinic reception or emailing no-reply@amarayurveda.in.
